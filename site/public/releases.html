What's new

What each version of the app brings.

Version 1.1

Coming soon

  • The Qur'an: the whole mushaf, page by page, with a night page for reading in the dark.
  • Ten reciters, verse by verse, at the speed you like, also with your screen locked or offline.
  • See the Qibla through your camera, and watch Makkah live.
  • Midnight and the last third of the night with the prayer times.
  • Widgets for today's times, a verse of the day, your reading and your tasbih.
  • The next prayer with a countdown on your Lock Screen.
  • Share a page, a verse or today's times as an image.
  • A simpler Settings screen, and easier to use with VoiceOver and larger text.

Version 1.0

13 November 2026

  • Prayer times that follow your mosque's method, with a monthly timetable and the Hijri date.
  • A Qibla compass, and a map with mosques nearby.
  • Suhoor and iftar times in Ramadan, with an iftar countdown on your Lock Screen.
  • The special days and nights of the Islamic year.
  • Reminders for every prayer, Jumu'ah, and suhoor and iftar.
  • Ask Siri when the next prayer is.
  • A tasbih counter, and a tracker for fasts and missed prayers.
  • In English, Dutch, German, French, Turkish and Arabic.
