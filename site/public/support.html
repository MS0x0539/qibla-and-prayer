Common questions

The times differ from my mosque's timetable.

Mosques use different calculation methods, mostly for Fajr and Isha. In Settings, choose the method your mosque uses: each one shows today's Fajr and Isha, so you can compare them with your mosque's timetable. Check the Asr setting too, and if a time is still a few minutes off, move it under Adjust times.

The Hijri date is a day off.

The app follows the Umm al-Qura calendar, or Diyanet's calendar when you use the Diyanet method. Where you live, the month can start a day earlier or later because of moon sighting. You can move the Hijri date by a day or two in Settings.

The compass points the wrong way.

Hold your iPhone flat, away from metal, magnets and other electronics, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass. Allow location access, so the compass can use true north instead of magnetic north.

I don't get notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your iPhone's Settings, that the bell next to the prayer is on, and that no Focus mode is holding them back. Open the app now and then: iOS keeps a limited number of reminders waiting. The app schedules the next days each time you open it, and now and then in the background if Background App Refresh is on for it.

The iftar countdown doesn't appear on my Lock Screen.

Start it yourself: from the Ramadan card on the Prayer times tab, or in the Tracker after turning on “I'm fasting today”. It can start from 8 hours before iftar, and Live Activities must be allowed for the app in your iPhone's Settings.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Nothing is sent to me. Place names, city search and the map use Apple Maps, and recitations come from Islamic Network; the privacy policy has the details.
