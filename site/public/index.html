Wajh: Qibla & Prayer Times

Prayer times and the Qibla, without ads or tracking.

Prayer times for 17 calculation methods, a Qibla compass and map, Ramadan times, a tasbih and a tracker for fasts and missed prayers. In English, Dutch, German, French, Turkish and Arabic.

  • No ads, no subscriptions, no account.
  • Nothing is collected about you, and nothing is sent to me. Your settings, tasbih and tracker stay on your iPhone; place names, city search and the map come from Apple Maps, and recitations of the Qur'an from Islamic Network.
  • Times follow the method of your mosque or authority and were checked against the timetables authorities publish. You can move each time by a few minutes to match your mosque.
